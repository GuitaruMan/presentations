Day 1꼭 알아야 할 단어 ①

1
notice[노우티스]동사
알아차리다
I notice people using smart phones.
2
spend[스펜드]동사
(시간·돈을) 쓰다
Don't spend too much time on games.
3
agree[어그리]동사
동의하다
I agree with you.
4
share[셰어]동사
나누다, 함께 쓰다
We can share our interests.
5
download[다운로드]동사
내려받다
I like downloading games and movies.
6
save[세이브]동사
아끼다, 저축하다
It tells us how much money we can save.
7
carry[캐리]동사
가지고 다니다
I always carry my cell phone.
8
cause[코즈]동사
일으키다
Cell phones might cause some problems.
9
consider[컨시더]동사
곰곰이 생각하다
Consider the advantages of them.
10
realize[리얼라이즈]동사
깨닫다
She seems to realize the problem.
11
rely[릴라이]동사
믿고 의지하다
People rely on their gadgets.
12
communicate[커뮤니케이트]동사
이야기를 나누다
I communicate with my friends.

Day 2꼭 알아야 할 단어 ②

13

관심, 흥미

16

17

A GPS application is very useful.
18
advantage[어드밴티지]명사
좋은 점, 장점
Consider the advantages of cell phones.
19
function[펑션]명사

Cell phones have many functions.
20
brain[브레인]명사
뇌, 머리

21

22

23
useful[유스풀]형용사

24
convenient[컨비니언트]형용사
